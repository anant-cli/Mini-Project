1. Our story

Why a parking platform, and what it actually changes for drivers and owners.

Finding a safe, convenient parking space is hard — especially near colleges, offices, shopping areas, and other crowded corners of a busy city.

ParkSlot brings drivers and parking-space providers onto one platform, so instead of driving in circles, users can search available spaces, check the details, and reserve the one that fits.

For space owners, it's a way to turn a driveway, garage, or unused commercial lot into something that actually earns its keep.

3. How ParkSlot works

Search → Compare → Reserve → Check-In → Park.

1. Search & filter

Drivers search by destination and requirements, then narrow the list.

  • Vehicle type
  • Availability & location
  • Price & EV charging
  • Parking duration

2. Reserve your space

Review the details and lock in a booking that includes:

  • Location, date & time
  • Vehicle type
  • Price & booking status

3. Check-in & park

On arrival, drivers verify the reservation through the booking/check-in system and park.

4. Manage your space

Hosts stay in control of what they've listed:

  • Availability & pricing
  • Timings & vehicle types
  • Bookings & earnings

Privacy Policy

ParkSlot collects and processes information that is necessary to provide parking, booking, payment, check-in, and account-management services.

1. Account Information

When a user creates an account, ParkSlot stores information such as the user's name, email address, phone number, password hash, account role, account creation date, and account update information. ParkSlot may also maintain information about whether an account has been verified or suspended.

2. Booking Information

When a user acts as a Driver, ParkSlot may collect information required to complete a parking Booking, including the vehicle number, selected Parking Slot, Booking start and end time, check-in and checkout time, check-in method, QR check-in token, estimated amount, total amount, overtime amount, and Booking status.

3. Parking Location Information

For Hosts and Business Hosts, ParkSlot stores information associated with the parking locations they provide. This may include the parking location name, address, latitude and longitude, number of available slots, pricing, supported vehicle types, EV charging availability, operating hours, photos, verification status, and other parking-space information.

4. User Activity and Transaction Information

ParkSlot may also process information related to user activity on the platform, including favorites, ratings and reviews, disputes, payment records, and Booking history. This information helps ParkSlot operate the platform, provide customer support, manage disputes, maintain accurate Booking records, improve services, and help protect the platform from misuse or fraudulent activity.

Important privacy note: ParkSlot stores latitude and longitude for parking locations in order to help Drivers discover suitable parking spaces. ParkSlot does not maintain a separate table or record of a user's live, real-time GPS location. Location data associated with a Parking Location should not be understood as, or represented as, a permanent record of a user's personal or live location.

5. Payment, Check-in, Location and Data Sharing

ParkSlot processes certain information between Drivers, Hosts, Business Hosts, and the platform when required to complete parking services. For example, when a Driver makes a Booking, information necessary to manage that Booking may be associated with the Driver, Parking Slot, Parking Location, and Host.

6. Payment Information

For payments, ParkSlot's systems record the payment amount, payment mode, payment status, payout status, gateway reference, and transaction time. ParkSlot does not store a complete card number or card security code (CVV) in its database. Sensitive payment credentials are handled through the applicable third-party payment gateway rather than being stored directly by ParkSlot.

7. Check-in Information

For check-in, ParkSlot stores the check-in time, checkout time, check-in method, and QR token associated with a Booking. Available check-in methods include QR code scanning, geofence-based automatic check-in, and manual override. This information may be used to verify that a Driver's Booking is being properly used.

8. Location Information

ParkSlot may use information about Parking Locations, including address, latitude, and longitude, to help Drivers discover suitable parking spaces. Location information belonging to a Parking Location is distinct from, and should not be confused with, a user's personal or live device location.

9. Data Sharing

ParkSlot may share information with relevant users only when reasonably necessary to provide the Service. For example, a Host may need Booking-related information to manage a parking reservation, while a Driver needs information about the Parking Location and Parking Slot to complete the Booking. ParkSlot may also disclose information where required for payment processing, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, or the protection of users and the platform.

Data ParkSlot does not collect: Based on ParkSlot's database structure, ParkSlot does not collect or store the following, unless separately collected by another part of the application or a third-party service:
  • Aadhaar number
  • PAN number
  • Full credit/debit card number
  • CVV (card security code)
  • Bank account number
  • A user's permanent live GPS history

10. Third-Party Services

ParkSlot may rely on third-party services for payment processing, hosting, analytics, email delivery, maps, authentication, or other platform operations. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

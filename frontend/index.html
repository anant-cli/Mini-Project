Frequently Asked Questions

How does the escrow payment protection work? ▼
When you book a spot, your payment is held in escrow. Funds are only released to the host after you successfully check in and complete your parking session, ensuring complete protection against unavailable or misrepresented spots.
Can I charge my Electric Vehicle (EV) while parked? ▼
Yes! Look for listings marked with the EV Charging badge. You can filter search results specifically for Type-1, Type-2, or CCS fast chargers, with transparent per-kWh billing.
What happens if someone else is parked in my reserved slot? ▼
You can raise an instant Dispute directly from your Bookings tab. Our admin support system immediately investigates, manually reviews and refunds your escrow payment if valid, and redirects you to a nearby backup slot.
How do host payouts work? ▼
Earnings accumulate in your Host Dashboard after every completed driver checkout. Payouts are transferred automatically according to your payout preference minus the standard platform commission.

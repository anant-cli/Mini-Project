4. Roles on the Platform

ParkSlot recognises four roles, each with distinct responsibilities.

4.1 Drivers

Drivers search for parking, make bookings, complete QR check-in on arrival, and pay for the slots they reserve. Drivers are responsible for arriving within their booked time window and following any rules set by the Host for the location.

4.2 Hosts and Business Hosts

Hosts list Parking Locations and Slots, set availability, and receive payment once a booking is completed. Hosts are responsible for the accuracy of their listings, including Vehicle Type support and EV Charging availability, and for keeping Slot Status up to date.

4.3 Admin

Admin oversees the platform, including user accounts, listing quality, dispute resolution, and enforcement of these Terms.

5. Bookings

A Booking is confirmed once payment is authorised and the Host's slot is marked as reserved for the requested time. Booking Status will move through Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, or Completed as the reservation progresses.

A Slot's Availability reflects its real-time state — Available, Booked, Occupied, or Disabled — and may change if a Host updates it directly.

6. Payments and Escrow

Payment is the amount charged to a Driver for using a parking slot or related service, such as EV Charging. Where Escrow Payment applies, funds are held by ParkSlot until the booking is completed or the applicable service conditions are met, at which point funds are released to the Host.

6.2 Hosts and Business Hosts

Hosts list Parking Locations and Slots, set availability, and receive payment once a booking is completed. A Host who exceeds two active listings or two total slots is automatically reclassified as a Business Host and may become subject to additional commercial obligations disclosed in-app. Hosts are responsible for the accuracy of their listings, including vehicle-type support, EV charging availability, and keeping slot status up to date.

6.3 Admin

Admin oversees the platform, including user accounts, listing quality, identity-verification review, dispute resolution, and enforcement of these Terms.

7. Bookings

A Booking is confirmed once payment is authorised and the Host's slot is marked as reserved for the requested time. Booking status moves through Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, or Completed as the reservation progresses.

A slot's Availability reflects its real-time state — Available, Booked, Occupied, or Disabled — and may change if a Host updates it directly.

At present, payments on this deployment are simulated for demonstration purposes only; no real money is transferred.
